📣 Scheduled Announcements

Write a message, pick a channel and a time, and the bot posts it for you: once, every day or every week. Daily and weekly posts keep the same clock time through daylight-saving changes. If the bot happens to be offline at the scheduled time, it posts the announcement late (once) as soon as it's back and marks it "posted late" here.
