Every violation deletes the message, saves an automod case (shown in Moderation and in /history), logs it and adds a strike. Strikes are stored in the database, so they survive bot restarts. No one bypasses AutoMod - not admins, not Manage Messages holders, not the owner - unless they have one of the exempt roles below. Exempt channels, bots and webhooks are never checked. The bot needs Manage Messages in the channel (plus Moderate Members / Kick / Ban for the strike ladder).
