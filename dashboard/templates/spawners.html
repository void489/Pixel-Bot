💬 Discord Commands

/spawner panelPost the price panel in the current channel. It keeps itself up to date.
/spawner add <type> <emoji>Add a spawner type, e.g. Skeleton 💀
/spawner setprice <type> <sell|buy> <price>Prices accept k / m / b: 8.2m, 8600000, 1.5b. Use "off" to stop a side. Open tickets are recalculated and their openers pinged.
/spawner remove <type>Remove a spawner type.
The commands stay available. Like every command, these are admin-only until you turn them on (and pick roles) on the Command Permissions page.

Add Spawner

"Spawners" is added for you.
Prices accept k / m / b (8.2m, 8600000, 1.5b). Changing a price recalculates open tickets and pings their openers. Pausing keeps the price: the panel shows it as price PAUSED and nobody can open a ticket for that side until you untick it. Open tickets are left alone. A minimum is the smallest amount a customer can order on that side (sell and buy are separate); an order or an amount change below it is refused. Staff can still set any amount on a ticket.
