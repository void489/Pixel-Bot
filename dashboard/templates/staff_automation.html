📋 Weekly staff report
One summary posted automatically each week: tickets handled and closed per person, new ratings, strikes given and expired, activity checks held, promotions and demotions, and who is on LOA. Taskbook counters have no history, so the bot saves a snapshot each week and reports the difference: the first report shows running totals and real weekly numbers start from the second. If the bot is offline at the scheduled time it posts late (as long as it's within a day).
Uses the saved channel. A "post now" report doesn't affect next week's numbers.
