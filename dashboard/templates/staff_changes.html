ℹ️ How it works

/promote <user> [reason]Gives the next role up. The old role is kept.
/demote <user> [reason]Removes their highest ladder role.
Accepted applications post a "Promoted" card automatically when the roles they give move someone up the ladder. Both commands are admin-only until enabled on Command Permissions.
