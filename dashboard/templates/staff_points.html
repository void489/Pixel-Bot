📥 Import past blacklists

Blacklist requests accepted before staff points existed aren't counted. This adds them once: each accepted add request pays the staff member who submitted it, dated by when it was accepted. Denied requests, unblacklist requests, requests approved by their own submitter and submitters who aren't staff right now are skipped. It's safe to click again: anything already counted is never paid twice.
