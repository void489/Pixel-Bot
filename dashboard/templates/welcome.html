Ping on Join

Hold Ctrl/Cmd to select several. Leave empty to turn Ping on Join off.
When someone joins, the bot pings them in each selected channel and deletes the message right away (a ghost ping), so they get the notification and the channel stays clean. Up to {{ ghostping_max }} channels. The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages and Manage Messages in each one. Bots that join are not pinged.

Auto Role

Hold Ctrl/Cmd to select several. Leave empty to turn auto role off.
Each role must be below the bot's highest role in Server Settings → Roles. Bots that join don't get auto roles, and roles with admin or moderation permissions can't be given out automatically.
